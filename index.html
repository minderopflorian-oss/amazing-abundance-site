Amazing Abundance

When energy, transport and labour become abundant, the cost of living falls

Solar, wind and batteries, self-driving electric vehicles and robots are arriving at the same time . Once built, they keep producing at close to zero extra cost . That is the start of an age of abundance, and I want to help build it .

Explore the three pillars

The idea

This vision builds on Tony Seba and James Arbib of RethinkX and their book Stellar : A World Beyond Limits, and How To Get There .

Their point is simple . For 10,000 years our economy has run on extraction : a constant flow of land, labour and capital to produce anything useful . A driver for every car, a trucker for every load, fuel burned for every kilometre .

Stellar technologies work differently . A solar panel needs materials once, then produces power for decades with no fuel and no driver . Seba calls them stellar because, like a star, once they reach their ignition point they just keep producing .

When energy, transport and labour all work like that, the basics of life get cheaper . Not as a promise of a date, but as a direction : and the people who prepare for it can help decide how it is owned and shared .

Three disruptions at once

  • Energy · solar, wind and batteries deliver clean power at near-zero marginal cost . RethinkX calls the surplus "superpower" . Global solar capacity has passed 3 terawatts, tripling in four years (RethinkX) .
  • Transport · autonomous electric vehicles turn a car into a service you call, not a machine you park . RethinkX expects Transport-as-a-Service to be four to ten times cheaper per mile than buying a new car (RethinkX) .
  • Labour · AI and robotics together become what Seba calls artificial labour : humanoids and robots that take on repetitive physical work in depots, warehouses and factories .

Together these form a new production system . Seba and Arbib argue it also needs a new organizing system : new business models and new ways of ownership, not old structures with new technology bolted on .

What it means

  • For people · getting around, moving goods and powering your home can cost a fraction of today . Less money and time spent on the basics, more left for everything else .
  • For cities · fewer parked cars, cleaner air and safer streets . Space that now goes to parking can go back to people .
  • For fleet owners · a robotaxi or an autonomous truck is a stellar asset : built once, it can serve many trips . The big question is who will own and run these fleets . I think that can be many people and local operators, not only a few large companies . That starts with learning together now .
