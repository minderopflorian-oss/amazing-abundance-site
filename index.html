Amazing Abundance

When robots drive, haul and generate, the cost of living falls .

Autonomy, robotaxis, humanoid robots and cheap clean energy are arriving at the same time . Together they can make the basics of life far cheaper : getting around, moving goods and powering our homes .

Explore the three pillars

The idea

For most of history, the things we need were scarce because they needed a lot of human time : a driver for every car, a trucker for every load, fuel burned for every kilometre .

That is changing . Software that drives does not get tired . Robots that work can run day and night . Sun and wind have no fuel bill . When the cost of labour and energy falls, the cost of almost everything built on top of them can fall too .

I call that an age of abundance . Not a promise of a date, but a direction : and the people who prepare for it can help shape how it is owned and shared .

What changes

  • Autonomy · cars and trucks that drive themselves turn transport into a service you call, not a machine you park .
  • Robotaxis · shared, electric and always available . Fleets can be owned by many people and run by local operators .
  • Humanoids · general-purpose robots take on repetitive physical work, from depots to warehouses .
  • Energy · solar, storage and smart charging make power cheaper and local, and fleets need a lot of it .
